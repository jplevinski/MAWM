About

M.A.W.M. is a country music artist delivering raw, honest storytelling through a catalog of over 30 original singles. From outlaw anthems to whiskey-soaked ballads, every track hits with authenticity and heart.

With releases spanning themes of love, loss, small-town life, and late-night revelry, M.A.W.M. brings a sound rooted in tradition but built for today.
